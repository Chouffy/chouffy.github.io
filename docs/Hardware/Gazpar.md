Is a connected [[gas]] counter used in [[France]]
## Notes on Teleinfo
- Connector: `MX44002SF1`, part of the JAE MX44 series
	- Female Connector 2 pin 1 row
	- 3.5 mm side pitch
- A button switch can be wired to the output to monitor for impulsion (1 impulsion per 10L), see [this thread](https://forum.hacf.fr/t/ajouter-la-consommation-gazpar-sur-longlet-energie/37977) and [this blog post](https://www.auto-domo.fr/home-assistant-recuperation-gazpar/)
